# Demo flow

## Target flow

1. Introduce the problem: children need space to practise thinking, not only recall.
2. Select Zara.
3. Start the market mission.
4. Show an age-appropriate generated question.
5. Let the learner answer.
6. Ask why and listen for reasoning.
7. Change the scenario or difficulty.
8. Ask the learner to reason again.
9. Show Learning Evidence as specific activity observations.
10. Briefly show Tobi's robot sequencing game.
11. Show Amara, David, and Favour as future possibilities.
12. End with the long-term vision.

## 90-second structure

- 0–15 seconds: problem and learning philosophy.
- 15–30 seconds: choose Zara and establish the market context.
- 30–60 seconds: mission, answer, explanation, and adaptive next challenge.
- 60–75 seconds: evidence from the session, using observation language rather than labels.
- 75–85 seconds: Tobi's deterministic game.
- 85–90 seconds: other worlds and future direction.

Keep the demo path seeded or rehearsed enough to remain reliable, while being honest about what is live AI and what is a prototype boundary.
